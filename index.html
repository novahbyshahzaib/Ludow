    How to play Ludo

    Goal: be the first to bring all 4 of your pieces home to the centre.

    • Tap Roll dice (or the dice, or Space). You need a 6 to move a piece out of base (turn this off in Settings).
    • Tap a glowing piece to move it. Pieces move clockwise around the board, then up your coloured home column.
    • You need an exact roll to finish — overshooting is not allowed.
    • Rolling a 6 gives an extra turn. Capturing an opponent or finishing a piece also gives an extra turn.
    • Three 6s in a row forfeits your turn (no move on the third 6).
    • Land on an opponent's piece to capture it — it goes back to its base. ★ safe cells (and coloured start cells) cannot be captured on.
    • Blocks: two or more of your pieces on one cell cannot be captured, and opponents cannot land there.
    • If you have no legal move, the turn passes automatically.
    • Pass & Play: hand the device to the next player. Vs CPU: the CPU moves on its own after a short think.
    • Dice: default is Fair Random using your device's cryptographic random generator — no algorithm favours anyone. Change it in Settings; dice stats on the game screen let you check fairness.

    Install Ludow

    Add Ludow to your home screen — it then opens full-screen, like a real app, and works fully offline.

    1. Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app.
    2. iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
    3. Desktop (Chrome/Edge): click the install icon in the address bar.
